⚡ CampusFix — Report. Track. Resolve.
Building a smarter, cleaner and more responsive campus by empowering everyone to Report, Track and Resolve.
CampusFix is an AI-powered smart campus complaint and issue-management platform designed to make reporting, routing, tracking, and resolving campus problems faster and more transparent.
Developed as a hackathon project by a CSE (AI & ML) student team, CampusFix combines structured complaint reporting, visual evidence, location information, AI-powered analysis, automated ticketing, and real-time status tracking into one unified workflow.
🚀 The Problem
Campus issues such as electrical faults, maintenance problems, infrastructure damage, cleanliness issues, and other facility-related complaints can be difficult to report and track through traditional processes.
CampusFix aims to solve this by creating a centralized digital complaint management system where students and staff can:
Report campus problems easily
Attach handwritten complaint evidence when required
Upload photos of the problem
Provide an exact location
Get AI-assisted analysis of the complaint
Receive a unique complaint/ticket ID
Track the complaint status in real time
Stay informed until the issue is resolved
💡 Our Solution
CampusFix follows a simple workflow:
Report → Analyze → Route → Track → Resolve
1. 📝 Smart Problem Reporting
Users can submit a campus complaint with:
Handwritten complaint/application evidence
Photos or visual proof
Detailed problem description
Exact location of the issue
This hybrid reporting approach allows traditional documentation and digital reporting to work together.
2. 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis
CampusFix analyzes the submitted complaint and helps generate:
Smart Classification — identifies the appropriate department
Priority Routing — determines the urgency of the issue
Executive Summary — converts the complaint into a concise overview for the maintenance team
Users can also review and edit the generated report before final submission.
3. 🎫 Instant Ticket Generation
After submission, CampusFix generates a unique complaint/ticket ID, providing an easy reference for future tracking.
The system also provides immediate confirmation after a complaint is submitted.
4. 📍 Real-Time Tracking
Users can follow the progress of their complaints through different stages, including:
Submitted → Checked In → Resolved
This improves transparency and keeps students and staff connected with the responsible team.
5. 🔐 Secure & Role-Based Access
CampusFix provides different access levels for:
Students & Staff
Administrators
The showcase also demonstrates secure account access and Google/Microsoft SSO integration.
